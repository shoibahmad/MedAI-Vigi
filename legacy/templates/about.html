PhenoRx

An Intelligent System for Automated Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) Identification

Our Mission

PhenoRx is an advanced clinical decision support system designed to predict and prevent Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs) using cutting-edge machine learning and artificial intelligence. Our mission is to enhance patient safety and improve clinical outcomes through intelligent pharmacovigilance, providing healthcare professionals with actionable insights at the point of care.

How to Use PhenoRx

Enter Patient Details

Start by providing basic patient information including name, age, gender, and clinician details on the patient details form.

Complete Clinical Assessment

Fill in comprehensive clinical data including demographics, physical measurements, laboratory values, comorbidities, and current medications.

Analyze Drug Interactions

Use the drug interaction checker to identify potential interactions between medications. The system provides severity ratings and clinical recommendations.

Calculate Personalized Doses

Access the personalized dosing calculator to determine optimal medication doses based on patient-specific parameters including renal and hepatic function.

Review AI Analysis

Examine the AI-powered clinical analysis and risk assessment. The system provides detailed insights, risk probabilities, and mitigation strategies.

Generate Medical Report

Create a comprehensive medical report that includes all assessment data, predictions, and recommendations. Reports can be downloaded as PDF for documentation.

Implement Recommendations

Follow the personalized mitigation strategies and monitoring protocols provided by the system to optimize patient safety and outcomes.
