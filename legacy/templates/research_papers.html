PhenoRx: An Intelligent System for Automated Adverse Drug Reaction Identification Using Machine Learning

Pharm.D Students, Integral University, Lucknow

2024
Integral University
Research Project

This research presents PhenoRx, an advanced artificial intelligence-powered system designed for automated identification and prediction of adverse drug reactions (ADRs). The system leverages machine learning algorithms trained on clinical data and integrates Google's Gemini AI for comprehensive clinical decision support. Our approach combines traditional pharmacovigilance methods with modern AI techniques to provide real-time risk assessment, drug interaction checking, and personalized dosing recommendations. The system demonstrates high accuracy in ADR prediction and offers valuable insights for healthcare professionals in clinical practice.

Integration of Pharmacogenomics in Clinical Decision Support Systems: A Case Study

Department of Pharmacy Practice, Integral University

2024
Integral University
Pharmacogenomics

This study explores the integration of pharmacogenomic data into clinical decision support systems for personalized medicine. We demonstrate how genetic variations in drug-metabolizing enzymes (CYP450 family) and drug transporters can be incorporated into dosing algorithms to optimize therapeutic outcomes and minimize adverse effects. The PhenoRx system serves as a practical implementation of these concepts, providing healthcare professionals with actionable pharmacogenomic insights at the point of care.

Real-Time Drug Interaction Detection Using AI: Improving Patient Safety in Polypharmacy

Clinical Pharmacy Research Group, Integral University

2024
Integral University
Drug Interactions

Polypharmacy is a significant challenge in modern healthcare, particularly in elderly patients with multiple comorbidities. This research presents an AI-powered approach to real-time drug interaction detection and severity assessment. Our system analyzes complex medication regimens and provides evidence-based recommendations to prevent potentially harmful drug combinations. The integration of natural language processing and machine learning enables the system to continuously update its knowledge base with the latest clinical evidence.
