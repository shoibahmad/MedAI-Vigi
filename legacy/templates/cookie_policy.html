Cookie Policy

Last Updated: November 2025

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners. Cookies help us understand how you use PhenoRx and improve your experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

PhenoRx uses cookies and similar technologies for several purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly
  • Session Management: To maintain your session and remember your preferences
  • Performance: To understand how you interact with the application
  • Functionality: To remember your settings and preferences

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Session Cookies Maintain your session while using the application. Store temporary data like patient information and assessment results. Session (deleted when browser closes)
Preference Cookies Remember your settings and preferences such as language, display options, and interface customizations. Persistent (up to 1 year)
Analytics Cookies Help us understand how users interact with the application to improve functionality and user experience. Persistent (up to 2 years)
Security Cookies Protect against fraudulent activity and ensure secure access to the application. Session or Persistent

4. Browser Storage Technologies

In addition to cookies, PhenoRx uses other browser storage technologies:

  • Local Storage: Stores data locally in your browser for improved performance and offline functionality
  • Session Storage: Temporarily stores data during your browsing session, including:
    • Patient assessment data
    • Prediction results
    • Form inputs
    • User preferences

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on PhenoRx are set by third-party services we use:

  • Google Gemini AI: May set cookies when processing AI analysis requests
  • Content Delivery Networks (CDNs): Used to deliver fonts, icons, and other resources efficiently
  • Analytics Services: May use cookies to track usage patterns (if implemented)

These third parties have their own privacy policies and cookie policies. We recommend reviewing them.

6. Managing Cookies

You have several options for managing cookies:

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Cookies and site permissions

Cookie Preferences

You can choose to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies
  • Delete cookies after each session
  • Block third-party cookies

7. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable cookies, some features of PhenoRx may not function properly:

  • Session data may not be preserved
  • Your preferences may not be remembered
  • Some features may be unavailable
  • You may need to re-enter information more frequently

Essential cookies are required for the application to function. Disabling them may prevent you from using PhenoRx effectively.

8. Data Protection and Privacy

Cookies and stored data are subject to our Privacy Policy. We take appropriate measures to:

  • Protect cookie data from unauthorized access
  • Use encryption where appropriate
  • Limit data collection to what is necessary
  • Comply with applicable data protection laws

9. Healthcare Data Considerations

When using PhenoRx for patient care:

  • Be aware that patient data may be temporarily stored in browser storage
  • Clear browser data when using shared computers
  • Use private/incognito browsing mode for sensitive cases
  • Follow your institution's policies regarding patient data
  • Ensure compliance with HIPAA and other healthcare privacy regulations

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in:

  • Our use of cookies and tracking technologies
  • Legal requirements
  • Best practices in data protection

We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

11. Your Consent

By using PhenoRx, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy. If you do not agree with our use of cookies, you should adjust your browser settings or discontinue use of the application.
