Complete guide to using PhenoRx ADR Intelligence System

Introduction

PhenoRx is an advanced AI-powered system designed for automated adverse drug reaction (ADR) identification and clinical decision support. This system leverages machine learning and Google's Gemini AI to provide comprehensive drug safety analysis.

Developed by Pharm.D students at Integral University, Lucknow, this system aims to assist healthcare professionals in making informed decisions about drug therapy and patient safety.

Key Features

AI-Powered Analysis
Advanced machine learning models trained on clinical data for accurate ADR prediction
Drug Interactions
Real-time drug interaction checking with severity levels and recommendations
Clinical Support
Comprehensive clinical decision support with personalized dosing recommendations
Pharmacogenomics
Advanced pharmacogenomic analysis for personalized medicine
Risk Assessment
Detailed risk stratification with probability scores and confidence intervals
Medical Reports
Generate comprehensive medical reports with AI insights and recommendations

Getting Started

1. ADR Risk Assessment

To perform an ADR risk assessment:

  1. Navigate to the Home page
  2. Fill in patient demographics (age, gender, weight, height)
  3. Enter medication information
  4. Select relevant medical conditions and risk factors
  5. Click "Assess ADR Risk" to generate predictions

2. Drug Interaction Checker

To check for drug interactions:

  1. Go to the Drug Interactions page
  2. Add medications one by one with their doses
  3. Click "Check Interactions" to analyze
  4. Review severity levels and recommendations

3. Clinical Decision Support

Access advanced clinical tools:

  1. Visit the Clinical Support page
  2. Choose from personalized dosing, pharmacogenomics, or TDM tools
  3. Enter required patient data
  4. Review AI-generated recommendations

System Architecture

PhenoRx is built using:

  • Backend: Python Flask framework
  • Machine Learning: Scikit-learn for ADR prediction models
  • AI Integration: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash for clinical insights
  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript with responsive design
  • Data Processing: Pandas, NumPy for data analysis

Data Input Requirements

Patient Demographics

  • Age: Patient age in years (0-120)
  • Gender: Male or Female
  • Weight: Body weight in kilograms
  • Height: Height in centimeters

Medication Information

  • Drug Name: Generic or brand name
  • Dose: Dosage amount and unit
  • Route: Administration route (oral, IV, etc.)
  • Duration: Treatment duration

Clinical Parameters

  • Renal Function: Creatinine, eGFR
  • Hepatic Function: AST, ALT, bilirubin
  • Comorbidities: Existing medical conditions
  • Concomitant Medications: Other drugs being taken

Output Interpretation

Risk Levels

  • Low Risk (<30%): Standard monitoring protocols
  • Medium Risk (30-70%): Enhanced monitoring recommended
  • High Risk (>70%): Intensive monitoring required

AI Analysis

The Gemini AI provides detailed clinical insights including:

  • Risk factor analysis
  • Mechanism of potential ADRs
  • Monitoring recommendations
  • Alternative therapy suggestions
  • Patient-specific considerations

Best Practices

  • Always verify patient information before assessment
  • Include all concomitant medications for accurate interaction checking
  • Review AI recommendations with clinical judgment
  • Update patient data regularly for ongoing monitoring
  • Document all assessments in patient records
  • Use the system as a decision support tool, not a replacement for clinical expertise
Important: This system is designed to assist healthcare professionals. All predictions and recommendations should be validated by qualified medical personnel before implementation.
